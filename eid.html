🍹 Party Cocktail Menu 🍹

Margarita

Tequila, lime juice, triple sec

Shake with ice, strain, optional salt rim.

Mojito

Rum, lime juice, mint, simple syrup, soda water

Muddle mint, add rum + ice, top with soda.

Piña Colada

Rum, pineapple juice, coconut cream

Shake with ice, strain, garnish with pineapple/cherry.
