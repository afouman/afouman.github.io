🍸 Cocktails

🍋‍🟩Margarita (Cooler)

Tequila, lime juice, triple sec

Shake with ice, strain into glass, optional salt rim. Perfect to batch in a cooler!

🌿Mojito (Made-to-Order)

Rum, lime juice, mint leaves, simple syrup, soda water

Muddle mint with lime & syrup, add rum + ice, top with soda water. Refreshing & fresh!

🥥Piña Colada (Made-to-Order)

Rum, pineapple juice, coconut cream

Shake with ice, strain into glass, garnish with pineapple/cherry. Tropical & creamy!
