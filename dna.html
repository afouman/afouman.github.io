What is Game of Life Simulator

PROVE THE WORLD THAT YOU CAN SUSTAIN A LIFE!!!

How the simulation works:

The simulation mimics basic life scenarios with some fundamental rules:

  1. Lifeforms can either survive on land, off land, or both environments (amphibious).
  2. When 2 lifeforms (dots) meet (touch), they create a new lifeform whose characteristics are derived from its parents + random mutation.
  3. After reproduction, parents and the newborn will go through a cool down period before reproducing again. Cool down period is controlled by the sliders on the right side.
  4. A newborn lifeform's life expectancy is between 1 second and 110% of the greater of its parents' life expectancies.
  5. Each lifeform has a cooldown period after reproduction during which it cannot to reproduce.
  6. If in-land or off-land lifeforms end up in an unsuitable environment, their lifespan decay 2x faster.
  7. If in-land and off-land lifeforms mate, their children can be in-land habitants, off-land habitants, amphibious, or unviable (death at birth).
  8. If the concentration of lifeforms exceeds 20 within a 3-dit radius, half will be eliminated due to disease outbreak or resource scarcity.

How to Play:
You get to design your own DNAs by double-clicking on this magical planet wherever you wish. Using the DNA Travel Speed, set the pace for the lifeforms to travel through this planet and eventually reproduce. As these lifeforms (dots) meet each other, they create a new life that is derived from the parents' DNAs + mutations. Learn what each nucleotide pair does to help you build the winning DNA.
